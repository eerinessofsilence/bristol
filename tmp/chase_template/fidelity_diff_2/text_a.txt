To use the template, simply go to the top of the page and click File -> Make a copy, and then you’ll have full edit access to the resume template. (How To Make A Copy)
A few things to keep in mind before you use it:Keep it to one page. Recruiters average 7 seconds per resume. Page two doesn't exist to them.
Lead with impact, not duties. Don't write what you did, write what you achieved. Numbers beat descriptions every time. "Reduced load time by 40%" beats "Responsible for performance optimization."
Tailor it for every role. Pull keywords directly from the job posting. ATS filters will quietly eliminate your resume if they don't match. Most candidates skip this and never know why they're not hearing back.
Don't underestimate your projects section. Especially early in your career, strong projects can unlock interviews that your work history alone won't. Use it.
One caveat: This template is built for software engineering and technical roles. If you're applying to non-technical positions, you may need to adjust the layout.
